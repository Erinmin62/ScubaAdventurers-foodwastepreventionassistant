# ExpiryGuardAI — revised food-operations product introduction

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Bring pantry, expiry, service records and planning together, then leave decisions with the owner.
- **Identity:** Persimmon, jade, warm rice-paper cream and deep indigo; Bricolage Grotesque / Figtree / JetBrains Mono; supplied leaf wordmark used unchanged.
- **Hero:** An expiry-label ring turns into a path through connected records, then resolves as an owner approval seal.
- **Motion:** Macro ring match cuts, a camera pull-back through a tangible data-flow world, then split-card folds that only close at human review.
- **Arc:** A daily planning problem → inputs connect → records become a bounded rules-based plan → a human decision is recorded → experiments stay fenced off → benefits remain expectations → logo and loop.
- **Payoff:** Operational records fold to the official lockup; the opening ring returns at the same position and scale.
- **Format / audience:** 16:9 landscape; cafes, restaurants, food vendors and cloud kitchens.
- **Runtime:** 42 seconds, seven scenes; unchanged from the existing film.
- **Truth boundary:** All interface rows, chart marks and proposal artwork are illustrative. No measured savings, accuracy, adoption, pilot result, food-safety validation or SDG impact is claimed.

## Variety / accuracy review

Seven scenes retain the original 42-second runtime, 16:9 frame and music. The visual grammar changes each beat: macro label, connected depth-world, ledger thread, planning chart, approval seal/activity rail, fenced research triptych, whitespace finale. The core flow starts with pantry/expiry batches and menu/recipe links, adds sales/preparation/leftovers, then reaches a rules-based plan and owner review. Daily planning and waste-aware reorder suggestions, hourly expiry checks, and post-approval follow-up are described without depicting automatic purchasing. Familiar structured fields are framed as learnability intent, not a proven outcome. Owner access, edit/approve/reject and searchable activity records are visible. Orbyte is only a potential fit; the app is described as standalone with no current integration. AI recipe drafts are owner-reviewed. Forecast experiments are distinct, temporary, advisory and unvalidated; owner-history uses archived sales/prep only after preview and one-request consent, excluding stock/current data. The local held-out check stays in the workspace and is not an AI validation. Leftovers are explicitly a proxy, not disposal weight/cost. Business and SDG benefits are expectations requiring pilot measurement, not outcomes.
