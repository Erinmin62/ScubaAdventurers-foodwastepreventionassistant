# ExpiryGuardAI — short product introduction

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Bring expiry, demand, and daily decisions into one clear view—while the business owner stays in control.
- **Identity:** ExpiryGuardAI's supplied visual identity: persimmon, jade, warm rice-paper cream, and deep indigo ink; Bricolage Grotesque / Figtree / JetBrains Mono.
- **Hero:** The transition from a physical expiry label to a legible owner workspace, with the pantry-risk panel and owner approval acting as the film's visual anchors.
- **Motion:** Label-to-interface match cuts, virtual-camera push-throughs between floating workspace panels, and drawn data paths that resolve only when an owner approves.
- **Arc:** A fast close-up hook becomes a measured product tour, then accelerates through the decision loop before a quiet, distinct brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The dashboard collapses into a small leaf mark inside a large rice-paper field; the opening circular expiry label returns as a matching loop handoff.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Audience:** Cafes, restaurants, food vendors, and cloud kitchens.
- **Runtime:** 42 seconds.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–6.00s / 6.00s — “A DATE CHANGES EVERYTHING.”

**Purpose:** Make the perishable-stock problem tangible in the first half-second, then introduce a calmer operational lens.

**Composition / artistic idea:** At open, an oversized circular jar-label graphic is already partly on-screen at x=72%, y=52%, diameter 82vmin, printed `BEST BY · 08 JUN` in a mono stamp. Cream field with dark ink type; a persimmon ring sits exactly on the label's edge. Huge type owns the left half, with a narrow safe inset. The label ring is the shot's physical carrier.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s label snaps from 0.88 to 1.04 and settles (stiff spring); eyebrow draws in by 0.20s. 0.35–0.70s `USE IT` punches in; 0.70–1.05s word swaps to `USE IT FIRST.` with an ink-red date stamp. 1.10–2.10s three tiny inventory ticks rotate around ring; one amber expiry tick lights at 1.75s. 2.2–3.1s value chip `RM 186` types into the ring center as an illustrative amount (clearly framed as risk, not saved money). 3.15–4.45s label scales back revealing faint, drawn pantry rows under it. 4.55–5.25s signature resolves. 5.25–6.00s ring accelerates forward to fill frame; type shears down-left and exits as the circle becomes a dashboard risk ring.

**Transition contract:** The exact persimmon circle at x=72%, y=52% becomes the circle/risk indicator in Shot 02. Compound handoff: circle scales through lens, while an ink panel unfolds from the left and the outgoing type shears away. No full-frame blank field.

**Assets / technique:** Brand palette and fonts from product CSS, CSS/SVG expiry label with stamped typography and ring. No external photography needed; subject is an operational system rather than a physical product.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental bed begins with a clipped, tactile beat; boundary accent at 6s was composed into the local fallback score. No VO or SFX requested.

## Variety / accuracy review

Seven beats progress through close material graphic, pulled-back product world, dark ledger close-up, centered chart, split approval moment, isolated disclosure frame, and open brand finale. Adjacent beats vary in scale, composition, field, and mechanic; no two consecutive scenes use the same layout. Every shot has timed changes through its final 15–20%, with transition movement instead of dead tails. Copy distinguishes the rule-based operational forecast, synthetic sample workspace data, owner-entered records, and the unvalidated AI lab. All amounts and chart bars are UI illustration, not outcome claims; no real forecast result is represented.
