# ExpiryGuardAI — food-operations product introduction

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Connect pantry expiry, service records, a rules-based plan, and owner decisions in one food-waste planning workspace.
- **Identity:** Supplied identity: persimmon, jade, warm rice-paper cream, deep indigo ink; Bricolage Grotesque / Figtree / JetBrains Mono.
- **Hero:** A circular expiry label becomes a connected operational workspace; the owner-review card is the decision anchor.
- **Motion:** Label-to-interface match cuts, a pull-back through floating operational panels, and drawn data paths that close only at owner review.
- **Arc:** Material hook → connected workflow → pantry/menu/service detail → explainable operational planning → owner-controlled action → sharply separated evaluation boundaries → quiet brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The workspace folds to the official leaf mark; the opening label ring returns for a loop handoff.
- **Format / audience:** 16:9 landscape; cafes, restaurants, food vendors, and cloud kitchens.
- **Runtime:** 42 seconds, seven scenes.
- **Records and UI:** All names, rows, charts, and proposal panels in the film are illustrative sample artwork; none depict owner records or verified outcomes.

## Variety / accuracy review

Seven consecutive layouts vary from material macro to depth-world workspace, dark ledger, cream chart, split decision card, isolated disclosure triptych, and whitespace finale. Each transition carries a ring, ledger, bar, check, or frame; the final 15–20% of each shot remains active handoff motion. The operational forecast is explicitly rules-based and uses recent workspace sales plus usable recipe stock. Recipe drafts are owner-reviewed. Routine planning, reorder proposals, expiry monitoring, follow-up, and ledger are not portrayed as autonomous purchasing. AI owner-history opt-in and the local rules-vs-prior-week check have different data handling and purposes, disclosed separately. No accuracy, savings, pilot, traction, safety, or performance claim is made. Every shown record and chart is illustrative sample artwork.
