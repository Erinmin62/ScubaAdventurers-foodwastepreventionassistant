---
name: SME financial priorities
description: Mentor feedback on prioritizing visible financial value for SME owners.
---

Prioritize financial impact in navigation, the Today overview and decision
presentation. Monetary savings should be large, clear and immediately visible
rather than a small secondary annotation.

**Why:** The user relayed mentor feedback that SME owners need to understand
the money impact quickly to make decisions.

**How to apply:** Preserve this priority when changing dashboards and recommendation
cards. Distinguish potential estimates, approved purchasing projections and
recorded waste comparisons; prominence must not turn estimates into claims of
realised savings.
